## Name

Bank Organiser

## Summary (10-250 characters, plain ASCII)

Sorts your bank into sensible tabs and puts each tab in order. Highlights what to move and where - you make every drag - with a plan you can fully customise and tabs you can lock.

## Description

Bank Organiser helps you turn a messy bank into tidy, sensible tabs, then put
the items inside each tab in order. It shows you what to do on your bank; you
drag every item yourself.

**How to use it**

1. Turn the plugin on in the Plugin Hub's Manage tab and open your bank.
2. Open the plugin window from its ribbon icon and click the all-items tab (the
   plugin points to it).
3. Follow the banner across the top of your bank. Items to move glow green,
   numbered in the order to drag them; drop them on the tab boxed in green (or
   the + button to start a new tab). Dragging in number order leaves the tab
   sorted.
4. When every item is in the right tab, the plugin helps you put your tabs in
   the plan's order (drag the green tab onto the blue one), then put the items
   inside each tab in order (drag the green item onto the blue one).

You can pause, skip a tab for now (it comes back once the rest are done) or click any tab in the list to work on it next. The plugin re-checks your bank
after every change, so you can stop and come back whenever you like.

**Two modes**

- *Organise my bank* follows a plan of up to 14 tabs (teleports and tools, a
  tab per combat style, food and potions, herblore and farming, gathering,
  crafting, archaeology, quest items and clues, cosmetics, everything else).
  It builds on your current tabs: tabs that already fit are kept, tabs that
  fit nothing are reused, and new tabs are only suggested when needed.
- *Just tidy my tabs* keeps your tabs as they are and only points out items
  that clearly belong in another of your tabs.

**Your bank, your rules**

- Lock any tab to leave it exactly as it is.
- Choose a plan style, then rename, reorder, add or delete tabs, choose which
  kinds of item each tab holds, and send items by name to a particular tab
  (type a name, or point at an item in your bank).
- Choose how many tabs to use, how small a tab can be, and whether to sort
  inside tabs.

**Helpful touches**

- Every tab gets an icon that shows what it holds (the best weapon for Melee,
  your best food for Food & potions...), and the plugin shows you how to set it.
- Numbered badges on your tab icons show which tabs are tidy.
- Point at any bank item to see which tab it belongs in.
- Items in your backpack show the number of the tab they belong in, so you can
  deposit straight into the right tab.
- After each drag, the banner tells you whether the item went to the right
  place.

Items are sorted using the game's own item information (Grand Exchange
category, combat style, item type), so the plugin knows its gear from its
food. Item names are matched in English, so a few items may be misplaced if
your game is set to another language.

## Data Protection (case 1)

This plugin uses the following information: the contents of your bank and backpack while your bank is open (item IDs, names, quantities and slot positions, and the game's item information), your bank tab settings, the item under your cursor while your bank is open, and your character's display name, used only to keep separate settings for each character.

This information is not transmitted outside of the plugin under any circumstances.

Game information is not stored by the plugin between sessions. Plugin settings, your tab plan and your tab locks are stored on your device between sessions using the plugin database, and can be deleted at any time from the plugin window (Edit plan > Delete saved data).
